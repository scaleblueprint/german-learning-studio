# Phase 2 — A1.1 lesson depth and visual consistency

Implemented: 23 October 2026.

The app remains aligned to **original Netzwerk A1.1 (Kurs- und Arbeitsbuch Teil 1)**, not *Netzwerk neu*. The user-provided 166-page scan was not accessible again in this work session. Alignment relies on the repository's earlier 166-page audit and the original publisher's public [Chapter 5 sample](https://www.klett-sprachen.de/downloads/2626/kursbuch-probekapitel-5-tag-fuer-tag/pdf).

The sample demonstrates photos for daily life, clocks, calendars, grammar comparisons and communicative exercises. Its protected artwork, pages, dialogues, recordings, answer keys and tasks have **not** been copied.

## Scope completed

- A shared worked-example component on the **Understand** stage for all twelve verified A1.1 guided lessons.
- Three original, topic-specific explanation-and-example cards per topic, followed by a common-form error comparison and interactive sentence ordering (36 cards and 12 challenges).
- Teacher Studio opens **the very same guided lesson** from each textbook chapter, then allows return to teaching. It reports locally attempted and mastered sentence labs.
- The existing two photo-to-phrase questions in each chapter now use only chapter-relevant phrase distractors rather than borrowing language from other chapters.
- The original grocery-list object clarifies **eine Packung Milch**, not ambiguous **eine Milch**.
- Accessible buttons, focus styling, responsive step cards and stored progress are included, with regression tests.

Chapters: (1) greetings, origin; (2) hobbies, professions; (3) places, directions; (4) food/shops, accusative; (5) time/routine, family/appointments; (6) birthdays/invitations, restaurant/past. The previous coursebook/workbook/Plattform ordering, exam practice and session builders are unchanged.

**Important remaining QA:** A German teacher must check all original language, register, inflection, examples, answer keys and lesson pacing. Photo availability and image-content accuracy require a real-device network/browser check. A1.2 chapters 7–12 remain outside the active path until verified with that edition's source PDF. This remains a single-browser demo; real student accounts and secure assignment tracking are not part of Phase 2.
